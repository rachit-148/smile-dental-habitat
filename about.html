Vaibhav Khand

Dr. Lokesh Chandra

BDS · Dentist, Dental Surgeon & Implantologist · 12 years in practice

Dr. Chandra founded Smile Dental Habitat in 2015 and has built its reputation on the cases most clinics prefer not to take — full-mouth rehabilitations, complex implant work, and reconstruction after facial trauma.

He keeps his practice current by exceeding the continuing-education requirement each year, and treats problems with the most modern techniques and equipment available to him. Patients most often come to him for root canal treatment, implants and clear aligners.

Paediatric Specialist

Dr. Saumya Taneja

BDS, MDS — Paedodontics & Preventive Dentistry · 15 years in practice, 11 as a specialist

Dr. Taneja is a paediatric dentist — a specialist in the teeth and mouths of children and adolescents. She took her post-graduation at King George’s Medical University, Lucknow, and has worked in hospitals of national importance including AIIMS.

Trained in conscious sedation and treatment under general anaesthesia, she is particularly experienced with children who have special needs and with those too anxious to sit through ordinary treatment. She also practises root canal treatment, aesthetic dentistry, crowns, bridges and veneers.

Orthodontist

Dr. Vikas Chauhan

BDS, MDS — Orthodontics · Braces & clear aligner specialist

Dr. Chauhan is an orthodontist — a specialist in moving teeth and correcting the bite. He plans and carries out the brace and clear-aligner treatment at Smile Dental Habitat, for teenagers and adults alike.

Straightening teeth is measured in months rather than appointments, so the plan, the likely timeline and the full cost are set out before anything begins, and progress is reviewed at every visit.

Our Story

Ten years in Indirapuram
