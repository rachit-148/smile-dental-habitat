Questions

Before you come in

Is a root canal painful?

Modern root canal treatment is done under local anaesthesia, and most patients describe it as no more uncomfortable than a filling. Our patients routinely report the procedure being completed in a single 25–30 minute sitting. The pain you feel beforehand — from the infected nerve — is usually far worse than the treatment itself.

I have lost most of my teeth. Can anything still be done?

Yes. Full-mouth rehabilitation using implants, fixed bridges or precision dentures can restore both chewing function and appearance, even when very few natural teeth remain. Much of our work at Smile Dental Habitat is exactly this kind of case. The first step is a consultation and an X-ray to assess bone levels.

How long do dental implants last?

A well-placed implant integrated into healthy bone is designed to be a permanent replacement. Longevity depends heavily on bone quality, oral hygiene and regular follow-up. We will give you an honest assessment of your particular case at consultation rather than a blanket promise.

What does a consultation cost?

₹500 at both clinics. This includes examination and a discussion of your treatment options. Any X-rays needed are taken in-house.

Which of your two clinics should I visit?

Vaibhav Khand (near Shipra Mall) is closest if you are in Vaibhav Khand, Gyan Khand, Abhay Khand, Vasundhara, Vaishali or Kaushambi. Ahinsa Khand-2 is closest for Ahinsa Khand, Nyay Khand, Niti Khand, Shakti Khand and the Noida side. Either can be reached on the numbers below.

Do you treat children?

Yes. Both clinics offer children's dentistry — preventive check-ups, fillings, extractions and habit correction, in an unhurried setting.

Do you accept cards and UPI?

Yes. Credit cards, debit cards and Google Pay / UPI are all accepted. The clinic is also wheelchair accessible.
